Groq is an AI inference platform designed to provide high-speed and low-latency inference for large language models. It utilizes its specialized hardware architecture, known as the Language Processing Unit (LPU), which is optimized for AI inference workloads. This architecture enables fast token generation and makes Groq particularly useful for real-time AI applications. Groq provides access to multiple supported AI models through its platform and API.
